# NOOR · نور — Art Direction
## "The Garden of Light" — an emerald & ivory Islamic wedding invitation

### Concept
A daylight wedding in a walled garden of light. White Makrana-style ivory
surfaces, deep emerald green, and quiet antique gold. The visual language is
Andalusian-Mughal refinement, NOT festival kitsch: fine geometric star
lattices, a single grand mihrab arch, slender fanous lanterns, white roses and
jasmine, olive leaves. Everything matte, soft, spacious. Nothing busy, nothing
glittering, nothing literal-clipart.

### Palette
| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--ivory` | #F7F2E7 | page paper, warm ivory |
| `--paper` | #FBF8F0 | raised cards |
| `--emerald` | #0B4A3C | primary deep green (headings, seals) |
| `--emerald-deep` | #063228 | night sections, envelope liner |
| `--gold` | #A9812F | antique gold lines, ornaments |
| `--gold-soft` | #C7A55B | soft gold fills |
| `--champagne` | #E7D7B0 | pale gold washes |
| `--rose` | #9C4A38 | warm terracotta-rose accent (dates, script) |
| `--ink` | #232B26 | body text on ivory |

### Type
- Display: **Marcellus** (quiet Roman caps — carries the "engraved plaque" voice)
- Editorial: **Cormorant Garamond** light italic for body poetry
- Script: **Pinyon Script** for the couple's names
- Arabic / ayat: **Amiri** (classical naskh, dignified, never decorative-fake)
- UI/body: **EB Garamond**

### Sections (in order)
1. **Envelope** — ivory envelope, emerald liner with fine gold star lattice,
   gold seal (8-point khatim star), address panel. Opening = flap lifts.
2. **Hero** — grand mihrab arch PNG crowning a tall ivory card; hanging
   lanterns at the shoulders; Bismillah in Amiri above the arch apex;
   names inside the arch; ayat panel (Ar-Rum 30:21) beneath the names.
3. **The Ayat** — full-width emerald-deep band, gold frame, 30:21 in Amiri
   with English translation beneath.
4. **Our Story** — two-column ivory, olive sprays, drop cap.
5. **Save the Date** — emerald band, gold star medallion, calendar buttons.
6. **The Celebrations** — timeline of Muslim events (Mehndi / Nikah / Walima
   / Dua), arch icons, emerald numerals.
7. **The Particulars** — two-column ivory card.
8. **The Palette / Attire** — emerald, ivory, gold, pearl swatches.
9. **Venue** — arch-framed, map link.
10. **Countdown** — emerald-deep band, gold numerals.
11. **Kindly Respond** — RSVP card, ivory.
12. **Closing** — crescent-and-star seal, dua (Barakallahu lakuma) in Amiri,
    gold rule, names.

### Rules
- Decorations are premium transparent PNG art (cutout pipeline), never
  stretched SVG clip-art. Small geometric marks may be inline SVG.
- Arabic text is real webfont text (Amiri) — never AI-generated calligraphy
  images (letterform errors are disrespectful).
- No stars-as-confetti overlays, no neon glows, no 3D bevels.
